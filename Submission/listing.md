## Description (40-4000, 1903 used)

Walk into a sports hall with eight table tennis tables, umpire desks with live flip scoreboards and a crowd in the stands, and play as your own HopLobby avatar. Up to 16 players play at once, two to a table (four in doubles), and the computer fills any empty seat so you can start alone and friends can drop in at any time.

The host picks the mode and the rules in the lobby. Singles is first to 7, 11 or 21 with the usual table tennis rules: two serves each, win by two. Doubles is 2 v 2 with partners taking turns to hit and the serve rotating the way the real game does. Tournament is a knockout bracket for up to 16 players: every round's matches play at the same time on their own tables, and the bracket fills in as results come in. Computer skill is Easy, Medium, Hard or Rising, which gets tougher every round.

Every shot is timed. A ring on the table shows where and when the ball can be reached, and you swing as it closes: hit it dead on for a PERFECT, slightly off for GOOD, and a late or early swing weakens the shot. How you swing decides the shot: aim left or right, choose short or deep, add pace for a smash, a push for backspin, or a lob to reset a hard rally. Keep it on the table, and keep your opponent guessing.

Controls. On a computer: Space (or Enter) swings as the ring closes; the arrow keys or WASD aim left and right and choose depth (up for deep, down for short); hold Shift for a hard hit; Z pushes with backspin; C lobs. On a phone or tablet: swipe up as the ring closes, a quick tap plays a short push, swipe down for a lob. Aim follows the direction of your swipe. The ring, HUD size and left-handed paddle are all in HopLobby's Esc menu.

Host players run the whole hall, so a match carries on if anyone leaves: a computer takes their seat, and if the host leaves another player takes over without a restart. Graphics follow HopLobby's presets, from Potato to Ultra.

## Notes for the reviewer (max 1000, 956 used)

No server of our own: the host's browser runs every table's match. It checks each swing against the sending player's own seat, the table's match id and the match clock, and drops anything malformed, forged or stale (tools/net-test.js sends junk and forged messages, 46 checks pass). No leaderboards, achievements, saves or store. Players are always their HopLobby avatars; computer players use bot models generated by our own script (no AI-generated assets). Sound effects are synthesised; music slots are empty. three.js r158 and its GLTFLoader (MIT) are bundled; the only outside script is the SDK from hoplobby.com. The HopLobby symbol appears only in the world (table halves, barrier boards, big screen), unaltered, never in the HUD. No localStorage. Settings and keys are in HopLobby's Esc menu; graphics follow HopLobby.graphics. Max 16 players fills all 8 tables.
